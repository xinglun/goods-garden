# Living Goods Model

## English

The product metaphor maps responsibilities as follows:

```text
Data       = Senses
AI         = Mind
Goods      = Living Entity
Store      = Habitat
Employee   = Caregiver
Enterprise = Ecosystem
```

These are product and domain-model mappings. They do not claim that AI has
actual emotion or consciousness. Words such as “happy”, “worried” and “in
pain” may later represent business states, but they are not claims about human
experience.

The model must remain grounded in evidence and authority. A metaphor cannot
grant an autonomous action permission.

## 中文

产品隐喻映射为 `Data = Senses`、`AI = Mind`、`Goods = Living Entity`、`Store = Habitat`、
`Employee = Caregiver`、`Enterprise = Ecosystem`。这是产品与领域模型的映射，不宣称 AI 具有真正的情感或意识。
“开心”“担心”“痛苦”未来可以表示业务状态，但不代表人的体验。隐喻不能授予自主行动权限，模型必须以证据和权限为基础。
