# Trust Model

## English

Phase 0 establishes principles only:

- Evidence over fluency.
- Unknown over fabrication.
- Human authority over inferred authority.
- Explainable Need.
- Traceable Care Action.
- Verifiable Outcome.

Information state values are:

```text
KNOWN | INFERRED | UNKNOWN | UNAVAILABLE | CONFLICTING
```

Every future product expression must trace to at least one of Observation,
Evidence, Inference or Human Input. A number without provenance is not enough
to justify a Need, Care Action or Learning claim.

There is no real SEJ data in this repository. POS schemas, inventory contracts,
replenishment rules, product KPIs and store operations are `UNKNOWN` unless a
later evidence-bearing Work Item establishes them.
